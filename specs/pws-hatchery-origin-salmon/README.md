# Prince William Sound hatchery-origin pink and chum salmon

Two stream-survey files record the sampling behind the estimates of hatchery-origin pink and chum salmon in 2013–2015. Each survey retains stream identifiers, specimen counts, marked and unmarked counts and the reported percentage of strays. [KNB dataset](https://knb.ecoinformatics.org/view/doi:10.5063/F1QV3JXF)

| Package table | Records | Original fields | Local record key |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| pink_stream_proportions | 485 | 15 | ADFGStreamCode, SurveyDate |
| chum_stream_proportions | 332 | 15 | ADFGStreamCode, SurveyDate |

Both selected stream-proportion files are complete. The publisher also deposits district indices, ocean surveys and model inputs. The selected rows support review of individual survey estimates and their denominators.

The published pink- and chum-survey entities are staged under the local names `pink-stream-proportions.csv` and `chum-stream-proportions.csv`. Their DataONE object identifiers and checksums remain those of the published entities.
